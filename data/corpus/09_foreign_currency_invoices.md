# Foreign Currency Invoice Processing

## 1. Currency agreement

The invoice currency must match the purchase order or signed contract. A supplier cannot change currency through invoice text alone. A mismatch requires a contract owner confirmation and Treasury review.

## 2. Conversion

Approval authority is assessed in AUD using the corporate daily rate for the invoice date. Posting uses the ERP-configured accounting rate. The record must cite the rate source, rate date and converted amount. Rates must not be invented or taken from an unverified attachment.

## 3. Variance

Exchange-rate movement is not a price variance when the invoice and PO share the same foreign currency. When a PO is in AUD and the invoice is foreign currency, the case must be held unless the PO explicitly permits conversion.

## 4. Overseas accounts

Payment to an overseas bank account is a higher-risk transaction under FIN-POL-003 and requires Financial Control co-approval. Sanctions screening must be current within 24 hours of the payment run.
