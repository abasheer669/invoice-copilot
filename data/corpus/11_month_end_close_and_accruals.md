# Month-End Close and Accruals

## 1. Cut-off

Invoices received by 3:00 pm local time on the third-last business day are targeted for the current close if evidence and approvals are complete. Missing a target does not permit control bypass.

## 2. Accrual evidence

An accrual requires evidence that goods or services were received before period end, a reasonable amount estimate, legal entity, cost centre and reversal date. A purchase order alone does not prove receipt.

## 3. Thresholds

Individual accruals below AUD 1,000 are not required unless they are recurring, intentionally split or material in aggregate. Accruals above AUD 100,000 require Financial Control review.

## 4. Agent use

An agent may identify candidate accruals and assemble evidence. It must distinguish recorded invoices from estimates and may not post a journal without approval. Every proposed amount must identify its calculation and source.
