# Credit Notes, Refunds and Overpayments

## 1. Credit notes

A credit note must reference the original invoice or contract and use the same vendor and currency unless Financial Control approves otherwise. It must not be treated as a negative invoice without validating tax and accounting treatment.

## 2. Application order

Valid credits are applied against open invoices for the same vendor and legal entity before a cash refund is requested. The application record must identify both documents and the remaining balance.

## 3. Overpayments

An overpayment must be placed in a recovery case. The processor must confirm the amount through ledger evidence and must not rely only on supplier correspondence. Recovery options are offset, refund to Northstar's verified account, or approved write-off.

## 4. Refund verification

Refund instructions sent to a supplier must use Northstar bank details obtained from Treasury's controlled directory. An agent may draft a request but cannot disclose full bank details in logs, prompts or test outputs.

## 5. Write-offs

Write-offs up to AUD 5,000 require a Department Director and Financial Control. Above AUD 5,000 requires CFO approval. Write-offs must not be used to conceal duplicate payments or unresolved fraud indicators.
