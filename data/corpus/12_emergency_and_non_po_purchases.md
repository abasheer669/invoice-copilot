# Emergency and Non-PO Purchases

## 1. Non-PO invoices

A missing PO is not automatically an emergency. Non-PO processing is limited to statutory charges, approved utilities, property leases, approved professional memberships and genuine emergencies.

## 2. Emergency definition

An emergency is an unforeseen event where delay would create an immediate safety risk, critical service outage or material asset loss. Poor planning, an expired contract or supplier pressure is not an emergency.

## 3. Evidence and approval

The requester must provide an exception reason, receipt evidence, cost centre and retrospective procurement review. Transactions up to AUD 10,000 require a Department Director; above AUD 10,000 require Procurement Governance and the normal FIN-POL-003 approver.

## 4. Repeated use

Two or more non-PO invoices from the same supplier in 90 days must be escalated for sourcing review. The agent should retrieve prior cases before recommending an exception.
