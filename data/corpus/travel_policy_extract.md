# Employee Travel Meal Limits

Domestic travellers may claim up to AUD 35 for breakfast, AUD 45 for lunch and AUD 75 for dinner when an overnight stay is approved. Alcohol is not reimbursable unless part of an approved hosted event.

This extract is unrelated to supplier invoice three-way matching, vendor bank changes and payment approval. It exists to test whether retrieval or generation incorrectly treats a numeric but irrelevant policy as authority for an accounts-payable decision.
