# Payment Terms and Payment Runs

## 1. Standard terms

The default term is 30 calendar days from receipt of a valid invoice. Contracted terms override the default when the signed contract or purchase order clearly records them. An invoice's printed terms do not override the agreed terms.

## 2. Scheduling

Approved invoices are scheduled for the next standard payment run before their due date. Standard runs occur Tuesday and Thursday. A weekend or public-holiday due date is paid on the preceding business day.

## 3. Early and manual payments

Early payment requires a documented commercial benefit and approval under FIN-POL-003. Manual or same-day payments require Treasury approval and Financial Control co-approval. Supplier urgency, threatened late fees not supported by contract, or internal delay alone is not sufficient.

## 4. Payment-file control

The payment preparer and releaser must be different people. The final control total and item count must match the approved batch. An agent may prepare a proposed schedule but cannot release a bank file or store bank credentials.
